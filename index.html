The Missing Trophy

Can you find it before tomorrow's assembly?

A quiet school. An empty trophy case.

The school day is over, and you are about to head home. The hallways are quiet. Mr. Lee is standing beside the trophy case, staring at an empty space.

"Do you know where the trophy is? It was here during the lunch break."

The trophy is needed for tomorrow morning's assembly. Posters on the wall announce that Mia is receiving an award, but the award itself is missing.

The glass is intact, and the other awards are still there. Somewhere in the school, there must be a clue. Every footstep echoes in the empty hallway.

Your task

  1. Search the hallway, classroom, library, and gym.
  2. Talk to Mr. Lee and Mia, and look for clues before jumping to conclusions.
  3. Find the trophy and return it to the case before you leave.

You can give up and go home, but then the mystery will remain unsolved.
